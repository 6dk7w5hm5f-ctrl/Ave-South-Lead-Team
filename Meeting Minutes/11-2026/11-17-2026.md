### Devotional: Emily
- Passage: John 10:11-18
- In the midst of times of reduced zeal, Jesus reminds us who He is.
- Jesus warns of “false guards” of the world that do not share the care for us that He does.
- No one was able to take Jesus’ life from Him, but out of His love for us, He willingly laid down his life.
- In the busyness of life it’s easy to depend on things that aren’t dependable. 
- Also, in the same way that Jesus cares for us who are in the fold, Jesus also seeks out those that are yet to be brought in.
- Eden: much of scripture is directed at the follower, but this also speaks to those that are still lost.
- Alec: Galatians 2:11-13
- Emily: Jesus speaks in such a way to be subverting expectations of not riding in to conquer, but to peacefully lay down His life, yet never diminishing His power over sin and death.
