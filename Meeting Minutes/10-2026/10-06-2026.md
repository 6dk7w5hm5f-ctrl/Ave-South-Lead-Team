### Devotional: Eli: being faithfully present to where god has you now
- Passage: Mark 1:21-38
- God’s calling on our lives isn’t just to our next step or place, but also to the place that God has us in right now.
- Often when Jesus traveled his journeys took longer than they “needed to” because Jesus was constantly stopping to minister to those around him.
- In v35-v38, though spending time to rest, Jesus knows that his purpose is to minister, and continues on to the next town.
- We should not get so caught up in the looking forward to what God has for us in the future that we forget that we forget to be ministering to those that He has placed around us.
