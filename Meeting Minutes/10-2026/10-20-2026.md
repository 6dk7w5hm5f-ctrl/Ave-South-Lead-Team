### Devotional: Ben
- The rebuilding of the temple after the Israelites returned from captivity was hindered by the peoples of the land.
- Once the rebuilding had begun, those that remembered the former glory of Solomon's temple were discouraged by the fact that the rebuilding effort did not live up to that former glory.
- God speaks to the people through Haggai, saying: 
- **Read Haggai 2:5-7** (Used CSB)
  - This is the promise I made to you when you came out of Egypt, and my Spirit is present among you; don’t be afraid.’” For the Lord of Armies says this: “Once more, in a little while, I am going to shake the heavens and the earth, the sea and the dry land. I will shake all the nations so that the treasures of all the nations will come, and I will fill this house with glory,” says the Lord of Armies.
- **Read Ephesians 2:4-5** (Used ESV)
	-  But God, being rich in mercy, because of the great love with which he loved us, even when we were dead in our trespasses, made us alive together with Christ —by grace you have been saved—
- Just as God did not ask the Israelites to bring His presence down but to build a place to host Him, it is not our job to do the work of the Lord in conforming us to be like him, but instead to live in such a way as to allow the Holy Spirit to do that work in us. If it were up to us to be like Jesus, we would be hopeless beyond a shadow of a doubt. But it is instead Christ who does that work in us, that we may be made right with him for eternity.
